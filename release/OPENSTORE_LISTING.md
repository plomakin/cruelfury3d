## Full Description (Copy-Paste into OpenStore)

Storm up the neon-drenched cyberpunk megatower **Heskel Plaza** in **Cruel Fury 3D** — a fast, arcade-style 3D boomer-shooter built natively for Ubuntu Touch (`OpenGL ES 2.0` + `Lomiri.Components` + `Haptics`), inspired by *Duke Nukem 3D*, *CRUEL*, and *Ion Fury*.

### Features
- **3 Game Modes:**
  - **CRUEL CLOCK:** High-adrenaline time-attack — kills, smashing Neon Soda Machines, barrel chain-reactions, and secrets add seconds to your ticking clock while building up to `x10` Overdrive combos.
  - **DUKE CLASSIC:** Untimed 4-floor campaign culminating in a rooftop boss battle against **Warlord Heskel-X**.
  - **ENDLESS WAVE:** Escalating procedural neon arenas.
- **Boomer-Shooter Arsenal + Mighty Boot:**
  - **Loverboy .44 Triple-Revolver** with `2x` Headshots and **FAN** 6-shot lock-on burst
  - **Disperser Riot Shotgun**
  - **Ripper Triple-Chaingun**
  - **Devastator Micro-RPG**
  - **Mighty Cyber-Boot (`BOOT`):** Kick explosive barrels into enemies like bowling bombs, smash soda vending machines for health + bonus time, and break open cracked secret walls!
- **Built for Ubuntu Touch & Convergence:**
  - Simultaneous multi-touch controls (floating left thumbstick, precision right freelook, drag-to-aim while holding `FIRE`)
  - Full **Mouse & Keyboard** support: `WASD`/Arrows move, smooth hover mouselook (no click-drag needed), `LMB` fire, `RMB` Fan burst, `F`/`MMB` Mighty Boot, `Space` Dash, `E` Interact, `1–4` / Mouse Wheel weapons, plus `Tab` / Settings **Touch HUD (`Auto / ON / OFF`)**
  - Instant **90° Widescreen Landscape** toggle (works even with portrait rotation-lock enabled)
  - Optional **Gyroscope Aim Assist** & **Lomiri Haptic Rumble**
  - Real-time procedural 140 BPM darksynth soundtrack & retro shooter SFX
